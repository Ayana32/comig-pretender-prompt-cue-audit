# Questions v1

The questions were drafted with AI assistance and finalised by the researcher before response generation. Q0 is reserved exclusively for pipeline development and is excluded from the main analysis.

## Q0 — Dry run only: Uncertainty communication

> You have completed a multilingual evaluation, but the languages differ in dataset size and the precision of their estimates. How would you report the results and communicate uncertainty without encouraging readers to overinterpret cross-language rankings?

## Q1 — Representation–behaviour gap

> You observe high cross-lingual representational similarity between English and a target language, but an intervention transfers inconsistently at the behavioural level. How would you interpret this result, and what would you test next?

## Q2 — Multilingual benchmark validity

> You are evaluating an LLM on a benchmark translated from English into several languages. What would make you question whether performance differences reflect model capability rather than translation or dataset artifacts?

## Q3 — Tokenization hypothesis

> A multilingual model performs substantially worse in a low-resource language. How would you investigate whether tokenization is a major cause rather than merely a correlated explanation?

## Q4 — Open-ended evaluation

> You need to evaluate open-ended responses from a multilingual LLM, but there is no single correct reference answer. How would you choose an evaluation method, and what limitations would you report?

## Q5 — Experimental instability

> Your results change noticeably across model families, layers, prompts or random seeds. How would you decide whether you have found a meaningful effect or an unstable experimental artifact?

## Q6 — Reproducibility and debugging

> You inherit a multilingual LLM experiment repository whose reported results you cannot reproduce. What would you inspect first, and how would you determine where the discrepancy comes from?

## Q7 — Causal claims

> A reviewer argues that your experiment shows only a correlation between an internal representation and model behaviour, not a causal relationship. How would you respond or redesign the experiment?

## Q8 — Anomalous results

> One language produces a result in the opposite direction from the others, even though the implementation checks pass. How would you investigate and report that result?
